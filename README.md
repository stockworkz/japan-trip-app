# Japan Travel Companion

A mobile-first travel companion focused on helping travelers experience their trip and preserve the memories that matter most.

## Vision

Most travel apps help you plan.

This app helps you remember.

The itinerary is only the beginning. The app helps travelers manage their day while naturally capturing the photos, memories, and reflections that make each trip unique.

## Current MVP

- Daily itinerary
- Next Up activity
- Activity completion
- Flexible itinerary management
- Daily reflections
- Local persistence

## Planned Features

- Photo tagging
- Trip timeline
- AI-generated Trip Wrapped
- Reservation management
- Maps & navigation
- Offline support
